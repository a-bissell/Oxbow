Read this before running anything

This system ranks on thermodynamic and electronic criteria computed from public databases. Deposition feasibility, film morphology, substrate compatibility and hygroscopic degradation under ambient handling are not modeled and must be assessed by the experimentalist.

Scores are relative within this run and profile. A candidate with a medium or low confidence label has criteria with no data behind them; they are named, not scored as zero. Band gaps marked “corrected” are scaled DFT values, not measurements. A shortlist entry is a conjecture; the caveats are the known counterexamples.
